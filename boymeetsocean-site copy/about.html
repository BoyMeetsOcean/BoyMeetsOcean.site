ABOUT ME

HEY, I’M ATTICUS.

I’m growing up aboard Liquid Sunshine, a 46-foot catamaran, with my family. Instead of one hometown, I’ve had anchorages. Instead of one classroom, I’ve had reefs, islands, weather systems, cultures, and the ocean.

I created Boy Meets Ocean to show the real side of growing up at sea—the incredible parts, the hard parts, and the funny parts nobody sees in a perfect travel photo.

I love freediving, fishing, filming, meeting people, exploring new places, and finding out what is just beyond the next point of land.

This site is where I’ll share longer stories, keep everyone updated on where we are, and build a collection of gear inspired by the adventure.
